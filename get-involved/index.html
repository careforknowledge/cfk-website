Join the CFK Fellowship

The Fellowship is CFK's primary volunteer community — structured contribution, community, and a defined advancement pathway. Applications open twice a year. Spring and Autumn cohorts.

Sprints are open to anyone with a laptop and the commitment to contribute for a full day. No fellowship required — just show up ready to work. Physical and virtual participation available.
