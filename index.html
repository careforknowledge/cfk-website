Our Mission

Quality education and skills training — regardless of location, income, or background.

CFK is a Nigerian nonprofit founded on the belief that the barriers between a student and a good education are not natural. They are built. And they can be dismantled — one open textbook, one free past question, one well-run simulation at a time.
