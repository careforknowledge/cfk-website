What to include in your message
For partnership enquiries
Tell us about your organisation, what kind of partnership you have in mind, and which of our programmes you are most interested in.
For press & media
Include your publication, your deadline, and what you are working on. We are happy to provide quotes and background.
For Fellowship enquiries
First check the Fellowship website at fellowship.careforknowledge.org — most questions are answered there. The Fellowship Companion is especially thorough.
