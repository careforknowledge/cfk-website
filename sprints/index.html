CFK Sprints

A full day. Real work.
Together.

CFK Sprints bring Fellows and interested volunteers together for a day of focused, high-output work on CFK's programmes — digitising past questions, writing textbook chapters, and moving the mission forward faster than any of us could alone.

Notify Me of Upcoming Sprints
What Is a Sprint?

A structured day. An open invitation.

A Sprint is a full-day working session — structured, focused, and productive. Participants work in parallel on CFK's programmes: some digitise past questions for WikiQuestions, others write or review textbook chapters for CTRI. By the end of the day, something real has been built.

Sprints are not closed to Fellows only. Any interested volunteer with the tools and the commitment to contribute for a full day is welcome. Bring a laptop. Bring focus. Leave having made something that will be used by students who need it.

Sprints have a physical presence — we host in Osogbo, Osun State — and virtual participation is available for those who cannot be there in person.

What you'll do
Digitise past questions, write textbook chapters, review and edit content, or support the day's logistics — depending on where you can contribute most.
What you'll need
A working laptop is a firm requirement for most Sprint activities. A willingness to focus for a full day. No prior experience with CFK required.
Physical & virtual
Sprints are hosted in Osogbo, Osun State. Virtual participation is available for those who cannot attend in person.
