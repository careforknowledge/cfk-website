Programmes

Five programmes.
One ecosystem.

Every CFK programme is designed to stand alone — and to be more valuable alongside the others. Together they form a complete, freely accessible learning infrastructure for Nigerian students.

The Ecosystem

How the programmes work together.

A secondary school student using CTRI textbooks can move directly to WikiQuestions for practice on the same topic — with video explanations at every step. A young Nigerian who participated in Model NASS might become a CFK Fellow. A Fellow who discovers a passion for writing might one day publish their first essay through First Essays. The programmes reinforce each other.
