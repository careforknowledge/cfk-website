Every WAEC past question.
Free.

WikiQuestions digitises, classifies, and explains WAEC past questions — building a free, topically organised practice resource aligned to CTRI textbooks.

Visit wikiquestions.org
Three Phases

From archive to ecosystem.

1
Digitise

Collect and publish WAEC past questions online — year by year, subject by subject. Free to access, download, and print the moment they are digitised.

2
Classify

Organise every question by topic — aligned to CTRI's chapter structure. Study a CTRI chapter, practise it immediately in WikiQuestions.

3
Explain

A video explanation for every question — volunteer-produced, one question per video. A world-class free resource that doesn't currently exist for Nigerian WAEC students at scale.
