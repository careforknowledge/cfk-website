Free textbooks.
Written for Nigeria.

Free, contextualised, conversational textbooks for Nigerian SS1–SS3 students preparing for WAEC. Written by Nigerians, for Nigerians. Published as open educational resources.

Visit ctri.ng
The Problem

Three barriers. One student. No good options.

The cost barrier
Quality textbooks exist. Many Nigerian students simply cannot afford them. This is an access problem, not a quality problem.
The context barrier
Free resources were largely written outside Nigeria — helpful, but not written for Nigerian students in Nigerian contexts.
The register barrier
Academic language is itself a barrier. CTRI textbooks are written in plain, conversational language — the way a good teacher talks, not the way a formal textbook reads.
CTRI's response
Free, contextualised, conversational open textbooks — written for Nigeria, published online, and distributed in print to the students the internet has not yet reached.
Two Phases

Online first. Offline next.

1
Write & Publish Online

Develop textbooks through structured writing, editing, and student review. Publish online as open educational resources — free to access, download, and print.

2
Print & Distribute Offline

Because CFK owns the copyright, we can print and distribute without fees. Phase 2 reaches students in hard-to-reach communities who cannot access the online resource.
