The Pathway

Where you start, and how far you can go.

6 months
Foundation Fellow

Entry level. Learn the community, find your contribution track, begin your fellowship journey.

12 months
Associate Fellow

Established contributor. Deeper programme involvement, growing community leadership.

18 months
Senior Fellow

Experienced leader. Mentoring Foundation Fellows, driving programme outcomes.

No limit
Principal Fellow

Highest level. Active for as long as engaged. Institutional memory and strategic leadership.
