First Essays

A publishing pipeline for
undergraduate writers.

First Essays helps Nigerian undergraduates discover the essay as a mode of thinking — from first draft to published work, through editorial feedback and peer review. We are building this now.

The Concept

Not a workshop. A publishing pipeline.

Many Nigerian undergraduates arrive at university capable and full of ideas — and discover they were never taught to write confidently as thinkers. First Essays is a structured pipeline from idea to published essay, with editorial feedback, peer review, and real publication under the writer's name.

Why essays matter
Understanding the essay as one of the oldest and most powerful modes of human thinking — not as an academic requirement, but as a practice to develop for life.
Writing and feedback
Draft, receive substantive editorial feedback, revise. The emphasis is on developing argument and voice — not conforming to a template.
Peer review
Experience peer review from both sides — a foundational introduction to how knowledge is evaluated in academic and intellectual life, which most Nigerian undergrads have never encountered as a lived practice.
Publication
Real publication, under the author's name, publicly available. For many participants, the first published work of their life. A credential and a beginning.
