First Essays

First Essays is something we are still building — a structured pipeline that takes Nigerian undergraduates from first draft to published essay, through editorial feedback and peer review.

🛠 This programme is not yet open. We are designing and testing it now. If you want to know when it launches, leave your details and we will reach you first.

The Concept

Not a workshop. A publishing pipeline.

Many Nigerian undergraduates arrive at university capable and full of ideas — and discover they were never taught to write confidently as thinkers. First Essays is designed to change that: a structured pipeline from idea to published essay, with editorial feedback, peer review, and real publication under the writer's name. We are still in the design and development phase, and we want to get this right before we open it.

Why essays matter
Understanding the essay as one of the oldest and most powerful modes of human thinking — not as an academic requirement, but as a practice to develop for life.
Writing and feedback
Draft, receive substantive editorial feedback, revise. The emphasis will be on developing argument and voice — not conforming to a template.
Peer review
Experience peer review from both sides — a foundational introduction to how knowledge is evaluated in academic and intellectual life, which most Nigerian undergrads have never encountered as a lived practice.
Publication
Real publication, under the author's name, publicly available. For many participants, the first published work of their life. A credential and a beginning.
