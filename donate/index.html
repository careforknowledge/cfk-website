How to Give

Give once. Give regularly. Both matter.

A one-time donation helps right now. A recurring donation gives CFK the ability to plan ahead — to know we can run a Sprint next quarter, host the next Model NASS session, or keep our platforms running without anxiety. If you can set up a recurring gift, even a small one, it changes what we are able to commit to.

Where It Goes

What your donation funds.

Platform & hosting
WikiQuestions, CTRI, Model NASS, and the Fellowship site all need infrastructure. Donations keep them online and running.
Sprints
Bringing volunteers together for a full day of focused work costs real money — venue, logistics, materials. Donations make Sprints possible.
Textbook development
Writing, editing, illustrating, and reviewing a textbook takes hundreds of hours. Donations help us compensate contributors fairly.
Distribution
Printing and distributing textbooks and past question sets to students in hard-to-reach communities requires resources. Donations fund Phase 2.
More Ways to Help
