About Us

We build what should have existed already.

Care for Knowledge is a Nigerian nonprofit. Our mission is universal access to quality education and skills training — regardless of location, income, or background.

Who We Are

A nonprofit built on a simple conviction.

Care for Knowledge was founded on the observation that the barriers between Nigerian students and a quality education are not natural. They are built — by cost, by geography, by the absence of materials that speak their context, by systems that were never designed to include them.

CFK exists to dismantle those barriers. We write free textbooks. We digitise and explain past questions. We run civic simulations that teach young Nigerians how governance actually works. We publish undergraduate essays. We build communities of volunteers who do this work together, for the long term.

None of this is fast. None of it is easy. But all of it is necessary — and we have chosen to do it.

Our Vision

A world where every student has the resources they need to learn — regardless of where they were born or what their family can afford.

Our Values
✦  Open by default — everything we build is free
✦  Contextualised — built for Nigeria, not adapted for it
✦  Community — we build with people, not for them
✦  Honest — about where we are and how far we have to go
